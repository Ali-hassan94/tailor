Shan Tailor - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how the Shan Tailor app ("the app") collects and uses information. The app is operated by Shan Tailor ("we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password (the password is stored securely by our authentication provider and is never visible to us).
  • Order details: information you enter when you place an order or booking, such as design, cloth type, measurements, notes and delivery information.
  • Photos: pictures of cloth or designs that you choose to attach to an order. Photos are optional.
  • Measurements: body measurements that our tailor may record for you in the shop.
  • Data saved on your phone: to let the app work offline, orders you create may be stored temporarily on your device until they can be sent.

How we use the information

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To prepare, stitch and deliver your orders and to contact you about them (for example by phone call or WhatsApp).
  • To show you the status of your orders.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertisements.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored with our service provider Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Your orders and photos can be seen by you and by the tailors of Shan Tailor. Other app users cannot see your data.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time inside the app: Profile > Delete my account. You can also request deletion by contacting us (see the account deletion page).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page.
