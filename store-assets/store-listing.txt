APP NAME (max 30):
Shan Tailor

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80):
Book tailoring orders, track stitching status and see latest designs.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Shan Tailor is the official app of Shan Tailor shop.

- Place tailoring orders for men's wear: shirts, suits, shalwar kameez and custom designs
- Add photos of cloth or a design you like (optional)
- Track your order status: Pending, In Progress, Completed
- Browse the latest designs from the shop
- See the delivery date and notes from your tailor
- Works even with weak internet: your order is saved and sent when you are back online
- Book alteration and stitching services

Stitching Style, Creating Confidence.
